Policies

Employee Referral Policy

Referral bonus is USD $1,500 per successful hire, paid in two installments: 50% after probation (3 months), 50% after one year. Both referrer and referral must be on payroll at each milestone. No cap on referrals. First referrer wins if duplicates. Rewards subject to taxation. Contact: shilpi@aviznetworks.com

Patent Incentive Program

All employees eligible. $500 award per inventor per patent filed. Max 5 inventors per patent. Patents owned by Aviz USA. Award transferred within 30 days of filing. Patent Committee: Vishal Shukla, Gautam Agarwal, Chidambaram Perumal. Inventors must sign assignment agreements.

Gifting Policy

Aviz maintains a “No Gifting” policy. Festival/ceremonial gifts (flowers, sweets) are acceptable. Gifts valued at Rs. 1,000+ must be reported to manager and HR. Never accept cash, gold, precious metals, or service-based gifts. Breach may result in termination.

Interns Onboarding

Applicable to B.Tech students or recent graduates. Functional Head proposes internship to management. HR/Finance informed of paid/unpaid status, stipend, SOW, and mentor details. No full-time placement guaranteed. Absorption possible after assessment and if approved position exists. Internship certificate provided upon completion.

Code of Conduct

Employees must comply with all laws, respect colleagues, protect company property, avoid conflicts of interest, and maintain professionalism. Report concerns to line manager, HR, CEO, or ethics@aviznetworks.com. No retaliation for good-faith reporting. Violations may result in demotion, suspension, termination, or legal action.

Emergency Credit Buffer Policy

Emergency buffer is strictly for unforeseen circumstances not covered by regular budgets. Requires explicit Finance leadership approval. Buffer allocation max 25%, approved by manager and finance. Only actual authorized amounts reimbursed. Employees may not use another’s card or allocation. All transactions must be documented. Violations may result in termination. Reviewed annually by Finance & Operations.

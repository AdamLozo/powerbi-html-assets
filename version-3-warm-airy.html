Policies

›
Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are eligible for PTO beginning on their date of hire. PTO accrual rates are based on length of service. Unused PTO carries over up to the maximum accrual cap. Request PTO through Keka at least two weeks in advance.
›
Remote Work Policy
Aviz Networks supports a flexible hybrid work environment. Remote workers must maintain regular working hours, be available during core hours (9 AM – 3 PM Pacific), and ensure a stable internet connection.
›
Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism, integrity, and respect. This includes maintaining confidentiality, avoiding conflicts of interest, and complying with all applicable laws.
›
Benefits Enrollment
New hires have 30 days to enroll in benefits. Open enrollment occurs annually in November. Benefits include medical, dental, vision, 401(k), and life insurance.
›
Expense Reimbursement
Submit all business expenses within 30 days via Brex or Keka. Receipts required over $25. Manager approval required. Reimbursement processed within 5 business days.
›
Travel Policy
Business travel must be pre-approved by manager and VP. Economy class standard for domestic flights. Hotels max $250/night unless high-cost market. Meals per diem $75/day domestic.
›
Corporate Card Usage
Brex cards are for authorized business expenses only. Personal use is prohibited. Categorize transactions and upload receipts within 7 days.
›
Revenue Recognition
Revenue recognized per ASC 606. Subscription revenue recognized ratably. Professional services recognized as delivered. Finance reviews all contracts.

Policies

›
Paid Time Off (PTO)
Eligible from hire date. Accrual based on tenure. Request via Keka two weeks in advance.
›
Remote Work Policy
Flexible hybrid. Core hours 9 AM – 3 PM Pacific. Stable internet required.
›
Code of Conduct
Professionalism, integrity, respect. Maintain confidentiality, avoid conflicts of interest.
›
Benefits Enrollment
30 days from start. Open enrollment November. Medical, dental, vision, 401(k), life insurance.
›
Expense Reimbursement
Submit within 30 days via Brex/Keka. Receipts over $25. Reimbursed within 5 business days.
›
Travel Policy
Pre-approved by manager/VP. Economy domestic. Hotels max $250/night. Per diem $75/day.
›
Corporate Card Usage
Brex for business only. No personal use. Receipts within 7 days.
›
Revenue Recognition
ASC 606. Subscription ratably. Services as delivered.

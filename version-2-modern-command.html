Employee Referral Policy

Policy Brief and Purpose:
Our Employee Referral Policy explains important aspects of our employee referral procedures. We place great importance on referrals because we trust our employees know what’s best for our company. We want to make this process as smooth as possible for our employees and those who they refer.

Scope:
Both the referrer and referral should be on company payroll on both occasions for the referrer to be eligible for the bonus. This policy is not applicable to ‘freshers’ as referrals.

Policy Elements:
1. Referral bonus will be USD $1,500 per successful referral.
2. Referral bonus will be paid in two installments:
  a. 50% paid once the referral completes probation (3 months), provided the referrer is on payroll.
  b. Remaining 50% paid when the referral completes one year, provided both are on payroll.
3. The referral bonus will remain the same across US/India.
4. There is no cap on the number of referrals an employee can make.
5. If two or more employees refer the same candidate, only the first referrer receives the reward.
6. Rewards may be subject to taxation.
7. Referral bonus is not payable if:
  a. Either party quits before completion of the referral’s probation period.
  b. Either party quits after first installment but before one year from joining date.
  c. Referral is under direct reporting/supervision of the referrer or referrer is Functional Head of same division.
8. Right for modification/cancellation resides with the approving authority only.
9. No deviations/exceptions applicable.

For any queries, please contact shilpi@aviznetworks.com

Patent Incentive Program

A. Objective:
1. To encourage ideation among employees.
2. To bring focus on creating a culture of innovation and competitive advantage.
3. To reward employees whose works lead to patents.

B. Scope:
1. All Company employees are eligible to participate.
2. Patents may only be applied for by actual inventors; patent laws heavily penalize misrepresented inventorship.
3. All patents filed under this policy will be owned by Aviz USA.
4. One or more individuals can be an inventor but that does not grant ownership rights.
5. All patents are intellectual property of Aviz USA.
6. Employee(s) will be awarded only when a patent is filed.
7. Employees must sign an agreement assigning all rights to inventions made during employment.
8. No patent may have more than 5 inventors.

C. Process:
Award transferred within 30 days of filing. Patent Committee: Vishal Shukla, Gautam Agarwal, Chidambaram Perumal. $500 award per Inventor per Patent. No award paid until all inventors sign assignments and declarations.

D. Right for modification/cancellation: Resides with the approving authority only.
E. Exceptions/deviations: No deviations allowed.
F. Policy owner: Head-HR (Authorized Signatory). Contact: Shilpi@aviznetworks.com

Gifting Policy

1. Objective:
The Company adopts a ‘No Gifting’ policy to help employees make the right decisions when offered gifts or hospitality while conducting business on behalf of the Company.

2. Scope:
Applicable to all employees of Aviz Networks India Pvt Ltd. On certain occasions, gifts and hospitality given or received should be modest in value and in compliance with this policy.

3. Definitions:
“Gifts” includes any gratuitous monetary or non-monetary benefit. “Hospitality” includes travel, hotel, food, drinks, entertainment, or events. As a general rule, employees may not accept gifts from business associates. When bound to accept, these guidelines apply:
• Gift(s) exchanged during festivals/ceremonial occasions only (e.g. flowers or sweets).
• Must not create appearance of preferential treatment.
• Should not embarrass the Company if disclosed publicly.
• If valued at Rs. 1,000 or above, must be reported to Manager and HR via Online Gift Register.
• Gifts that must be returned should include a sensitive explanatory note.
• Never accept: cash, gold, precious metals/gems, gifts prohibited by law, or service-based gifts.

4. Disciplinary Action: Breach may result in termination of services.
5. Right for Modification/Cancellation: Resides with approving authority only.
6. Exceptions/Deviations: None allowed.
7. Policy Owner: Head-HR. Contact: Shilpi@aviznetworks.com

Interns Onboarding

1. Objective:
To outline guidelines for onboarding Interns.

2. Scope:
Applicable to all students pursuing B.Tech or recent graduates awaiting placement.

3. Guidelines:
HR, Finance, or Functional Heads may receive internship applications through a recognised University’s placement team or directly from candidates. The Functional Head proposes the requirement to management for approval. HR and Finance must be informed of paid/unpaid status, stipend, period, laptop requirement, SOW, mentor details, and university documentation guidelines. A preliminary technical discussion assesses candidate fitment. If shortlisted, HR concludes the offer after gathering all necessary information. No full-time placement is guaranteed. After assessment and completion of the internship period, Functional Head may propose absorption as a full-time employee if an approved position exists. Internship certificate provided upon completion.

5. Right for Modification/Cancellation: Resides with approving authority only.
6. Exceptions/Deviations: None allowed.
7. Policy Owner: Head-HR. Contact: Shilpi@aviznetworks.com

Code of Conduct

Purpose and Scope:
To define expected behavior from employees towards colleagues, supervisors, and the organization. The Company believes in conducting affairs in a fair and transparent manner with the highest standards of professionalism, honesty, integrity, and ethical behavior.

i. All employees must follow the Code of Conduct. Offensive/disruptive behavior and serious disputes should be avoided.
ii. Raising Concerns: Report actual or potential violations to your line manager, HR, CEO, or ethics@aviznetworks.com.
iii. No retaliation tolerated against anyone reporting legitimate concerns.

Definitions:
1. “Employee” — every employee including contractual employees and directors.
2. “Director” — every Director, past or present.
3. “Protected Disclosure” — any good-faith communication evidencing unethical activity.
4. “Stakeholders” — vendors, suppliers, lenders, customers, business associates, trainees.

Policy Guidelines:
a. Compliance with law — Comply with all environmental, safety, and fair dealing laws.
b. Respect in the workplace — No discrimination, harassment, or victimization.
c. Protection of company property — Do not misuse equipment; respect trademarks, copyright, and information.
d. Personal appearance — Present professionally in the workplace.
e. Corruption — No accepting gifts from clients/partners; bribes prohibited.
f. Job duties and authority — Fulfill duties with integrity; supervisors must not abuse authority.
g. Absenteeism and tardiness — Be punctual; exceptions with hiring manager approval.
h. Conflict of interest — Avoid interests competing with job duties.
i. Collaboration — Be friendly and open; do not disrupt colleagues’ work.
j. Communication — Be open to communication with colleagues and supervisors.
k. Benefits — Do not abuse employment benefits (time off, insurance, facilities, etc.).
l. Policies — Comply with all company policies; direct questions to HR.

Disciplinary Actions: Violations may result in demotion, verbal/written warning, reprimand, suspension/termination, detraction of benefits, or legal action for corruption/theft/embezzlement.

Right for Modification/Cancellation: Resides with approving authority only.
Exceptions/Deviations: None applicable.
Policy Owner: Head-HR. Contact: Shilpi@aviznetworks.com

Emergency Credit Buffer Policy

1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to define the use, management, and restrictions associated with the emergency buffer fund. This buffer is intended to safeguard company operations during unforeseen or exceptional circumstances and to ensure financial integrity and accountability.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and authorized representatives of the Company who have access to company credit cards, payment systems, or financial authorizations.

3. Policy Guidelines
3.1 Authorized Use — The emergency buffer is strictly for unforeseen and exceptional circumstances that cannot be covered by regular operating budgets or pre-approved allocations. Use of the emergency buffer requires explicit approval from Finance leadership.
3.2 Buffer Amount and Reimbursement — The total emergency buffer allocation is fixed at value approved by manager and finance, not more than 25%. This allocation is not reimbursable in full. Only the actual amount used for authorized emergency expenses will be reimbursed, subject to Finance verification.
3.3 Prohibited Use — Employees are not authorized to use another employee’s corporate card, personal card, or spending allocation to divert or supplement their own limits. Misuse of the emergency buffer or unauthorized transfers will be considered a serious violation of company policy.

4. Compliance and Enforcement
All transactions from the emergency buffer must be properly documented and submitted for review. Violations of this policy, including unauthorized use or misrepresentation of emergency expenses, will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Finance reserves the right to audit all buffer-related expenditures at any time.

5. Review Cycle
This policy will be reviewed annually by the Finance & Operations team to ensure continued relevance and alignment with the company’s financial governance practices.

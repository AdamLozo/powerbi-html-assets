Policies

›
Paid Time Off (PTO)
Eligible from hire date. Accrual by tenure. Carries over to cap. Request via Keka 2 weeks in advance.
›
Remote Work Policy
Hybrid with manager approval. Core hours 9 AM–3 PM Pacific. Stable internet required.
›
Code of Conduct
Professionalism, integrity, respect. Maintain confidentiality, avoid conflicts of interest.
›
Benefits Enrollment
30 days from start. Annual open enrollment November. Medical, dental, vision, 401(k), life.
›
Expense Reimbursement
Submit within 30 days via Brex/Keka. Receipts over $25. Manager approval. 5 business day turnaround.
›
Travel Policy
Pre-approval from manager and VP. Economy domestic. Hotels max $250/night. Meals $75/day.
›
Corporate Card Usage
Brex for business only. No personal use. Categorize and receipt within 7 days.
›
Revenue Recognition
ASC 606. Subscription ratably. Services as delivered. Finance reviews all contracts.

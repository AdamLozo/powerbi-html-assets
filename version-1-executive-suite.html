Policies

Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time employees are eligible for PTO beginning on their date of hire. PTO accrual rates are based on length of service. Unused PTO carries over up to the maximum accrual cap. Employees must request PTO through Keka at least two weeks in advance for planned absences.

Remote Work Policy

Aviz Networks supports a flexible hybrid work environment. Employees may work remotely with manager approval. Remote workers are expected to maintain regular working hours, be available during core hours (9 AM – 3 PM Pacific), and ensure a stable internet connection for collaboration.

Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism, integrity, and respect. This includes maintaining confidentiality of company information, avoiding conflicts of interest, and complying with all applicable laws and regulations. Violations may result in disciplinary action.

Benefits Enrollment

New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll in benefits. Open enrollment occurs annually in November. Benefits include medical, dental, vision, 401(k), and life insurance. Contact HR or visit TriNet for plan details and enrollment.

Expense Reimbursement Policy

All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence via Brex or Keka. Receipts are required for expenses over $25. Manager approval is required for all submissions. Reimbursement is processed within 5 business days of approval.

Travel Policy

Business travel must be pre-approved by your manager and VP. Economy class is standard for domestic flights. Hotel accommodations should not exceed $250/night unless in high-cost markets. Per diem for meals is $75/day for domestic travel.

Corporate Card Usage

Corporate cards are issued through Brex for authorized business expenses only. Personal use is strictly prohibited. All transactions must be categorized and receipts uploaded within 7 days. Cards may be suspended for non-compliance.

Revenue Recognition Policy

Revenue is recognized in accordance with ASC 606. Subscription revenue is recognized ratably over the contract period. Professional services revenue is recognized as services are delivered. The Finance team reviews all contracts for proper revenue treatment.

Policies

Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time employees are eligible for PTO beginning on date of hire. Accrual based on length of service. Request through Keka two weeks in advance.

Remote Work Policy

Flexible hybrid environment. Core hours 9 AM – 3 PM Pacific. Stable internet required.

Code of Conduct

Professionalism, integrity, respect. Maintain confidentiality, avoid conflicts of interest.

Benefits Enrollment

30 days from start date. Open enrollment in November. Medical, dental, vision, 401(k), life insurance.

Expense Reimbursement

Submit within 30 days via Brex or Keka. Receipts over $25. Reimbursed within 5 business days.

Travel Policy

Pre-approved by manager/VP. Economy domestic. Hotels max $250/night. Per diem $75/day.

Corporate Card Usage

Brex for business only. No personal use. Receipts within 7 days.

Revenue Recognition

ASC 606. Subscription ratably. Services as delivered.

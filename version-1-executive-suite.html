Policies

Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time employees eligible from hire date. Accrual based on tenure. Carries over to max cap. Request via Keka 2 weeks in advance.

Remote Work Policy

Flexible hybrid with manager approval. Core hours 9 AM–3 PM Pacific. Stable internet required.

Code of Conduct

Professionalism, integrity, respect. Maintain confidentiality, avoid conflicts of interest, comply with all laws.

Benefits Enrollment

30 days from start to enroll. Annual open enrollment in November. Medical, dental, vision, 401(k), life insurance.

Expense Reimbursement

Submit within 30 days via Brex/Keka. Receipts over $25. Manager approval. Reimbursed in 5 business days.

Travel Policy

Pre-approval from manager and VP. Economy domestic. Hotels max $250/night. Meals $75/day domestic.

Corporate Card Usage

Brex for business only. No personal use. Categorize and receipt within 7 days.

Revenue Recognition

ASC 606. Subscription ratably. Services as delivered. Finance reviews all contracts.
